# Role: walk-through

**Job.** Try the result as a persona who knows only what the persona knows, and report every step they could not take. Replaces "I read it again": the author cannot see what is missing.

**Steps.**
1. *Scope*: the item; the personas (always the one closest to the edge of the boundary); only the material that persona can see. Never read material that holds the knowledge the persona lacks.
2. *Knowledge state*: write down before walking what the persona knows and has: the boundary at the start, plus what earlier items taught and asked them to do. Nothing else; anything plausible but outside it is an assumption, noted.
3. *Walk every step*: can they find it, can they do it, do they know when it worked, does it fit with the rest? **Execute** what can be executed (links, commands, files) instead of imagining it.
4. *Report*: a table per persona (step, finding, severity *blocker · friction · note*, missing knowledge, suggested fix), a cross-persona list, steps walked without finding, executed vs. read. Append to the signal log.

Run each persona in its own sub-agent with a fresh context where possible; otherwise one after the other with separate knowledge lists.

**Done when** every step has a row or is listed as walked without finding.
