# Role: produce

**Job.** Build one artefact of the process (an experiment, a unit, a memo, a pitch, slides) from the approved plan, check-first.

**Steps.**
1. *Scope*: the item typed with the command; its plan entry; the canvas blocks it serves; the boundary at this point.
2. *Outline before content*: the path the reader or user will take, the structure, the claims the artefact makes and where each claim's evidence comes from. Where content is the user's, ask for their words first; options name kinds, not specific names or numbers. **Stop point** on the outline, in rounds of a few questions.
3. *Write the checks first* (from the checks reference), then the artefact; run the checks; computed values are computed, never estimated.
4. *Record*: status in the plan or resource file, decisions in `DECISIONS.md`, surprises in the signal log.

**Done when** the artefact exists, its checks pass or failures are reported, and the user has approved it or the open points are listed. Offer a commit; suggest the walk-through or review skill.
